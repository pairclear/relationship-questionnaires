# What changed in how we keep in touch

[Take it online (runs in your browser, sends nothing)](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=contact-changes&lang=en)

> Self-reflection, not diagnosis. Written by PairClear, not validated in research. It measures how things go between two people, not anyone's psyche.

For when you feel a partner has become more distant. These questions are about what actually changed between you, not about what your partner feels: only they can answer that.

**Think about:** the last month, compared with before.

**Answer scale:** 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost always

| # | Statement | Part |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My partner starts conversations or texts less often than before. | Change in contact |
| 2 | Plans together are made later, vaguer or get cancelled more than before. | Change in contact |
| 3 | Replies to me are shorter or slower than they used to be. | Change in contact |
| 4 | We talk less about the future (holidays, plans, 'next year'). | Change in contact |
| 5 | When I bring up something important, my partner engages with it. | Change in contact (reverse-scored) |
| 6 | I check my phone waiting for a message. | How much it takes from me |
| 7 | I replay conversations looking for signs of what's wrong. | How much it takes from me |
| 8 | I avoid asking directly because I'm afraid of the answer. | How much it takes from me |

## Scoring

Reverse the marked items (4 becomes 0, 3 becomes 1). Average the items of each part to get a 0-4 score. Under 1.5 is low, 1.5 to 2.5 is in the middle, above 2.5 is high. These are not norms or cut-offs: the questionnaire is not validated. Use the result to start a conversation, not to judge.

## Parts

**Change in contact**

- low: Few visible changes. The distance you feel may be about something not yet said; asking directly is often easier than guessing.
- mid: Some things have changed. Pick one concrete change and ask about it calmly, without a conclusion attached.
- high: A lot has changed in how you keep in touch. That deserves a direct conversation about what is going on for both of you. Changes have many possible causes: stress, work, health, doubts.

**How much it takes from me**

- low: It doesn't take much of your attention. Good: you have room to think clearly.
- mid: It takes a noticeable part of your attention. Try writing down facts separately from interpretations.
- high: It takes a lot of your attention and energy. Whatever happens next, it may help to talk to someone you trust, or a psychologist, about how you are.

## Questions to think about

- Which change bothers you most? Describe it as a fact, without a conclusion.
- What else could explain it, apart from your first guess?
- What would you want to ask your partner, and what would you need to hear?
