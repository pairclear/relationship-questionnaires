# After the breakup: where am I now

[Take it online (runs in your browser, sends nothing)](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=after-breakup&lang=en)

> Self-reflection, not diagnosis. Written by PairClear, not validated in research. It measures how things go between two people, not anyone's psyche.

Not a prediction of whether they'll come back: nobody can make one. These questions show how much of your attention is still on the ex, and how much is coming back to your own life.

**Think about:** the last 2 weeks.

**Answer scale:** 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost always

| # | Statement | Part |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I check their social media or online status. | Still holding on |
| 2 | I reread old messages or look at old photos. | Still holding on |
| 3 | I plan what I'd say if they wrote to me. | Still holding on |
| 4 | I make decisions based on whether they'd notice. | Still holding on |
| 5 | I spend time with people who are good for me. | Coming back to my life |
| 6 | I do something just for myself, not to show anyone. | Coming back to my life |
| 7 | There are hours when I don't think about the breakup. | Coming back to my life |
| 8 | I can think about the future without the ex in it. | Coming back to my life |

## Scoring

Reverse the marked items (4 becomes 0, 3 becomes 1). Average the items of each part to get a 0-4 score. Under 1.5 is low, 1.5 to 2.5 is in the middle, above 2.5 is high. These are not norms or cut-offs: the questionnaire is not validated. Use the result to start a conversation, not to judge.

## Parts

**Still holding on**

- low: The ex takes up little of your attention now.
- mid: Part of your attention is still there. That's normal; try one small limit, like muting their profile for a week.
- high: A lot of your attention is still on the ex. Be kind to yourself, and if it's been months or it's affecting sleep and work, talking to a psychologist can help.

**Coming back to my life**

- low: Your own life hasn't come back yet. Start with one thing that used to be yours before the relationship.
- mid: Some of your life is coming back. Notice which days are easier, and what's in them.
- high: Your life is filling up again.

## Questions to think about

- What do you miss most: the person, the routine, or who you were with them?
- What is one thing you want back in your life that isn't them?
- If they wrote tomorrow, what would you want for yourself?
