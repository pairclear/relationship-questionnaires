# Who carries the relationship: effort balance

[Take it online (runs in your browser, sends nothing)](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=effort-balance&lang=en)

> Self-reflection, not diagnosis. Written by PairClear, not validated in research. It measures how things go between two people, not anyone's psyche.

About the early or undefined stage, when you are not sure both of you are putting in the same. It looks at what each of you does, not at intentions.

**Think about:** the last 4 weeks.

**Answer scale:** 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost always

## Scoring

Reverse the marked items (4 becomes 0, 3 becomes 1). Average the items of each part to get a 0-4 score. Under 1.5 is low, 1.5 to 2.5 is in the middle, above 2.5 is high. These are not norms or cut-offs: the questionnaire is not validated. Use the result to start a conversation, not to judge.

## Parts

**Imbalance of effort**

- low: Effort looks fairly shared.
- mid: You do noticeably more in some areas. Try stepping back once and see what happens, without testing or punishing.
- high: Most of the effort seems to come from you. That's worth naming directly: what you want, and what you'd need from the other person to keep going.

**Clarity between us**

- low: There is little clarity about what this is. You are allowed to ask.
- mid: Some things are clear, some are not. Which question would you most like answered?
- high: You mostly know where you stand.

## Questions to think about

- What would 'enough effort' from the other side look like, concretely?
- What are you afraid will happen if you stop carrying it for a week?
- What would you tell a friend in the same situation?
