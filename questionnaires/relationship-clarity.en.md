# Are we together or not? Relationship clarity

[Take it online (runs in your browser, sends nothing)](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=relationship-clarity&lang=en)

> Self-reflection, not diagnosis. Written by PairClear, not validated in research. It measures how things go between two people, not anyone's psyche.

For a 'situationship': you meet, maybe sleep together, but nobody has said what it is. These questions look at how much is clear between you and how the uncertainty sits with you.

**Think about:** since you started seeing each other.

**Answer scale:** 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost always

| # | Statement | Part |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We make plans more than a few days ahead. | Clear signals |
| 2 | I've met their friends, or they've met mine. | Clear signals |
| 3 | We've talked about whether we see other people. | Clear signals |
| 4 | When I bring up 'what are we', the topic gets changed or postponed. | Clear signals (reverse-scored) |
| 5 | I hold back from other options while waiting for this to become clear. | What the uncertainty costs me |
| 6 | I feel anxious when I don't hear from them. | What the uncertainty costs me |
| 7 | I pretend I'm fine with 'no labels' when I'm not. | What the uncertainty costs me |
| 8 | I've thought about ending it just to stop not knowing. | What the uncertainty costs me |

## Scoring

Reverse the marked items (4 becomes 0, 3 becomes 1). Average the items of each part to get a 0-4 score. Under 1.5 is low, 1.5 to 2.5 is in the middle, above 2.5 is high. These are not norms or cut-offs: the questionnaire is not validated. Use the result to start a conversation, not to judge.

## Parts

**Clear signals**

- low: Few clear signals of where this is going. You can't read it from behaviour alone; a direct question is the only reliable source.
- mid: Mixed signals. Write down what was actually said and done, separately from what you hope or fear it means.
- high: Things are fairly clear between you.

**What the uncertainty costs me**

- low: The uncertainty doesn't cost you much right now.
- mid: It costs you something. Decide for yourself how long you're willing to wait, before you talk.
- high: The uncertainty costs you a lot. Your needs count too; asking for clarity is not pressure.

## Questions to think about

- What do you actually want this to be?
- What answer are you afraid of, and what would you do if you heard it?
- How long are you willing to wait, and what happens after that?
