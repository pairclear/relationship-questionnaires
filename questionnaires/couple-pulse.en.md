# Couple pulse: a 2-minute weekly check-in

[Take it online (runs in your browser, sends nothing)](https://pairclear.github.io/relationship-questionnaires/?q=couple-pulse&lang=en)

> Self-reflection, not diagnosis. Written by PairClear, not validated in research. It measures how things go between two people, not anyone's psyche.

Eight quick questions about the last week in your relationship. Good to fill in separately and then compare over tea, not to win an argument.

**Think about:** the last 7 days.

**Answer scale:** 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Almost always

| # | Statement | Part |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We had at least one conversation that wasn't about chores, kids or logistics. | Connection |
| 2 | I felt my partner was interested in how my week went. | Connection |
| 3 | We laughed together. | Connection |
| 4 | I felt comfortable asking my partner for something I needed. | Connection |
| 5 | Small things turned into arguments. | Friction |
| 6 | I held back something I wanted to say to avoid a fight. | Friction |
| 7 | After a disagreement, one of us stayed cold for a long time. | Friction |
| 8 | When we disagreed, we managed to make up the same day. | Friction (reverse-scored) |

## Scoring

Reverse the marked items (4 becomes 0, 3 becomes 1). Average the items of each part to get a 0-4 score. Under 1.5 is low, 1.5 to 2.5 is in the middle, above 2.5 is high. These are not norms or cut-offs: the questionnaire is not validated. Use the result to start a conversation, not to judge.

## Parts

**Connection**

- low: Little sense of connection this week. Worth asking each other what one small moment together would look like.
- mid: Some connection, some distance. Notice which moments felt good and repeat one of them on purpose.
- high: You felt connected most of the week. Worth saying out loud what helped.

**Friction**

- low: Little tension this week.
- mid: Some tension. Is there one topic that keeps coming back? Name it, and pick a calm time to talk.
- high: A tense week. Before solving anything, agree on a short pause rule for arguments and a time to talk when both are calm.

## Questions to think about

- What was the best moment between you this week?
- What is one thing you'd like more of next week?
- Is there a topic you're avoiding? What makes it hard to raise?
